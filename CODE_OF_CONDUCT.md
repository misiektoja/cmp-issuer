# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy and focus criticism on technical work. Handle security reports and sensitive PKI material privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an established private channel. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately and respectful of reporter confidentiality.
